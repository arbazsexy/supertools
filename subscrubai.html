Welcome to SubScrubAI

SubScrubAI helps users manage and analyze their email subscriptions more efficiently. By connecting your Gmail account, SubScrubAI identifies subscription emails and provides insights about recurring services.

Our goal is to simplify subscription tracking and help users stay informed about their digital spending.

Features

Subscription Analysis

Detect and organize subscription emails automatically.

Smart Insights

AI-powered understanding of your recurring services.

Newsletter Detection

Identify unwanted or recurring email subscriptions.

Inbox Organization

Keep your subscriptions structured and manageable.

Install & Access App

To use SubScrubAI, install the Android app from Google Play and connect your Gmail account.

Install App Join Testing Program

Steps to use:
1. Install and open SubScrubAI
2. Tap "Connect Gmail"
3. Sign in with Google account
4. Grant Gmail readonly permission
5. View subscription dashboard

Privacy & Security

SubScrubAI only requests access required for core functionality. We do not sell or misuse user data.

Users can revoke Gmail access at any time from their Google Account settings.
